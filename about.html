OUR STORY

We're building the last website builder you'll ever need.

We got tired of watching good ideas die in design queues. ForgeAI exists because building a website still takes too long, costs too much, and depends on too many people saying yes.

Why We Started Forging

Collapse the distance between an idea and a live website.

Our mission: collapse the distance between an idea and a live, working website to under five minutes — without making it look generated. Every founder deserves a site that feels hand-built, forged with the same care a senior design team would bring, minus the six-week timeline and the invoice that comes with it.

Milestones

From a rough idea to 14,000+ live sites

A short history of a company that moves faster than its own roadmap.

2023

Founded

Three engineers, tired of watching clients wait eight weeks for a landing page, started prototyping a prompt-to-site generation engine in a shared apartment.

2024

Public Beta

ForgeAI opened its doors to 500 beta founders. The first 1,000 sites were forged in under three months — most launched same-day.

2024

Design System Engine

Shipped the live design-system generator — no more recycled themes. Every ForgeAI site got its own forged palette, type scale, and components.

Today

14,000+ Sites Forged

Founders, agencies, and solo builders across 60+ countries now ship production websites with ForgeAI before their coffee cools.
